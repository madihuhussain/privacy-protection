SpendWise MV Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 May 2026

SpendWise MV is a local-first personal finance tracking app created to help users track accounts, income, expenses, transfers, budgets, savings goals, recurring bills, and loans. This Privacy Policy explains how SpendWise MV handles information.

1. Information the app stores

SpendWise MV may store information that users enter into the app, including account names, balances, transactions, categories, budgets, goals, recurring expenses, debt or loan records, notes, images selected by the user for accounts or goals, app settings, and local quality analytics events.

2. Local storage

SpendWise MV is designed to store normal app data locally on the user's device or browser. The app does not require a user account or login to use its main features.

3. Backups and exports

SpendWise MV allows users to create backup or export files. These files may contain personal financial information entered into the app. Backup files are created only when the user chooses to export or download them. Users are responsible for keeping backup files private and secure.

4. Data sharing

SpendWise MV does not sell user data. The app does not share personal financial records with advertisers. If a user manually shares a backup file, screenshot, or exported file with another person or service, that sharing is controlled by the user.

5. Analytics

SpendWise MV may store simple local quality analytics events inside the app, such as whether a feature was used. These events are intended to help improve the app experience. They are stored locally and can be cleared by the user from inside the app, where available.

6. Permissions

SpendWise MV may request device permissions only when needed for app features, such as selecting images from the gallery, choosing backup files, saving files, or sharing backup/export files. The app uses these permissions only for the feature requested by the user.

7. Children's privacy

SpendWise MV is intended for personal finance management and is not directed to children under 13. The app should be used by adults or with appropriate supervision where required.

8. Security

Because SpendWise MV stores financial information locally, users should protect their device with a passcode, biometric lock, or other security protection. Users should also keep exported backup files private.

9. Data deletion

Users can delete records inside the app where deletion options are available. Users may also clear all local SpendWise MV data using the reset/clear data option in the app, where available. Removing the app from a device may also remove locally stored app data, depending on the platform.

10. Financial disclaimer

SpendWise MV is a personal finance tracking tool only. It is not a bank, wallet, payment provider, lending service, accounting service, investment service, or financial advisor.

11. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
